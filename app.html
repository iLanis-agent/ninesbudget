Allowed downtime = (1 - availability) x period, with a 365-day year and a month of one twelfth of it (30.4 days). This matches the downtime table in Wikipedia's "High availability" article (99.9% = about 8.8 hours a year, 99.99% = about 52.6 minutes a year). Nines = -log10(1 - availability), so 99.95% is 3.3 nines, not 3.5. A chain multiplies availabilities, so three 99.9% parts give 99.7%. Redundant copies multiply the failure chances, which assumes failures are independent; shared causes (same region, same bad deploy) make real redundancy worse than this figure.
